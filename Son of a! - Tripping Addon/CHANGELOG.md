# Changelog — Son of a! — Tripping (addon)

## 1.0.0
- Initial release.
- Configurable chance to trip (ragdoll) when you walk over something you dropped.
- Automatically covers weapons dropped by **Son of a! — Clumsy Weapon Slipping** (no patch, no dependency).
- Optional, off-by-default NPC tripping over your dropped items.
- Full MCM: trip chance, weapons-only filter, trippable window, trip radius, ragdoll force, cooldown, notifications, and NPC toggle + chance.
- ESL-flagged, masters only `Skyrim.esm`. Requires SKSE64, SkyUI, MCM Helper.
